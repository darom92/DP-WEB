A website for your business without spending money: is it possible?

When you are starting out, every euro counts. You need customers to find you, see what you offer and know how to contact you, but you may not yet be able to afford a professional website.

That is where Google Sites fits in: a free Google tool to create a free website for your business without writing a line of code. It does not work miracles, but for a first online presence, a page for your freelance service or a test of your idea, it can be a good starting point.

In this guide you will learn how to make a website on Google Sites from scratch and how to avoid the most common mistakes. We will also tell you honestly when it falls short, so you can decide with confidence.

Google Sites for entrepreneurs: who is it for?

It is a good option if you need to create a free website with Google Sites to present your activity and you don't yet have the budget for a custom site. It fits especially well in these cases:

  • A website for freelancers and independent professionals who need an online business card: physiotherapists, electricians, translators, consultants, photographers…
  • A free website for small businesses that want to show their services, opening hours and how to get there.
  • People who want to create a free website for my business before investing, to test whether their idea has demand.

If, on the other hand, you already have customers and need to sell online or compete for Google searches, you will probably outgrow it. We will cover that at the end.

What Google Sites is and what it is for

Google Sites is Google's website builder. It works in your browser, is saved in your Google account and nothing needs to be installed. It is free for personal accounts.

It is used to create simple, informational websites, for example:

  • A presentation page for a freelancer or independent professional.
  • A services website with your contact details and opening hours.
  • A portfolio showing your work.
  • A page for an event, a project or an idea you want to test.

Important: Google Sites is not an online store or a blogging platform. If you want to sell products or publish articles regularly, you will need another solution later on.

How to create a free website on Google Sites: step by step

This is the shortest possible Google Sites tutorial, step by step. We expand on each part further down.

  1. Sign in to Google Sites with your Google account

    Go to sites.google.com and sign in. If you don't have a Google account, create one for free. It is best to use an account you will keep long term, because your website will be tied to it.

  2. Create a new site

    Choose a blank site or a template from the gallery. If in doubt, start with a template: it gives you a structure you can change later.

  3. Name your site and your first page

    Use your business name as the site name. It is what visitors will see in the browser tab, so keep it clear.

  4. Choose a theme and customise colours and fonts

    In the Themes panel pick one of Google's themes and adjust the design so it matches your brand.

  5. Add your content

    From the Insert menu add text boxes, images, buttons, a map or a form. Start with a title, one sentence explaining what you do and a contact button.

  6. Create the rest of your pages

    In the Pages panel add About, Services and Contact. Further down we give you a recommended structure.

  7. Check it in the preview

    Click Preview to see how it looks on desktop, tablet and mobile. Fix anything that doesn't look right.

  8. Publish your website

    Click Publish, choose the web address and confirm. From then on, every change only appears after you publish again.

How to choose a template and customise the design

To design a free website that doesn't look improvised, start with the template. It sets the initial structure of your site. It is not a final decision: you can change the theme later from the Themes panel.

What to look for in a template

  • That it resembles your type of business. A portfolio, a professional service and an event need different structures.
  • That it leaves room for the essentials: what you do, who for and how to contact you.
  • That it is simple. Fewer decorations usually convey more trust.

How to customise the design

  • Choose one of Google's themes or create your own with your colours.
  • In Settings → Brand images you can upload your logo and favicon (the tab icon).
  • In Settings → Navigation you decide whether the menu goes at the top or on the side.
  • Use few colours (one main and one supporting) and a single typeface for body text.

Tip: use your own images or openly licensed ones. Real photos of your work build more trust than generic ones.

For more detail, see Google's official help on changing the theme and navigation.

How to add text, images, buttons and forms

Everything is added from the Insert menu in the right-hand panel. It is the easiest part of the process.

Text

Click Insert → Text box. Use the title and heading styles to organise information: it helps your readers and Google understand the page. Write short paragraphs and clear sentences.

Images

Click Insert → Images and choose a photo from your computer or from Drive. Google Sites supports common formats such as JPG, PNG and GIF. Before uploading, give them descriptive names (for example physiotherapist-valencia.jpg, not IMG_2034.jpg) and compress the heavy ones.

Don't forget the alt text: select the image, click More editing options → Add alt text and describe what is shown. It helps accessibility and helps Google understand the image.

Buttons

Click Insert → Button, type the text (for example, Book your appointment) and paste a link or choose a page on your site. You can pick a filled, outlined or text-only style. Use action verbs: Contact me, Book, Get a quote.

Contact form

Google Sites has no form of its own, but it connects with Google Forms:

  1. Create a form in Google Forms with the minimum fields: name, email and message.
  2. On your site, click Insert → Forms and choose the one you just created.
  3. Publish the site again and test it by sending yourself a message.

You can also embed a Google map, YouTube videos or Drive documents. Google's help has the full list of content you can add.

How to create the Home, About, Services and Contact pages

A simple website for a new business needs no more than four or five pages. Add them from the Pages panel.

Home

It should answer three questions within five seconds: what you do, who for and how to contact you.

Example for a freelancer

Title: Home physiotherapy in Valencia

Sentence: I help you recover from injuries at home, with personalised sessions.

Button: Book your first appointment

About

Tell people who you are, your experience and why you do what you do. Include a photo of yourself. People hire people, not websites.

Services

List what you offer with one sentence per service and, if you can, who it is for. End each service with a contact button.

Contact

Include the form, an email, your phone or WhatsApp, your work area and opening hours. It is the page where clarity is most appreciated.

And, if you like, a few more

  • FAQ: answer doubts and save messages.
  • Reviews or case studies: only if they are real and you have permission to show them.
  • Legal notice and privacy: if you collect data with a form, you need to explain how you handle it.

How to publish your website for free

  1. Click Publish at the top.
  2. Type the address you want. Your site will have a format like sites.google.com/view/your-business-name.
  3. Click Publish and open the address to check that it loads properly.

Who can see your website

Click Share and, next to Published site, choose Public if you want anyone to see it. If you leave it as Restricted, only people you invite will see it.

Remember: changes don't appear on the published site until you click Publish again. More details in Google's official help for publishing and sharing your site.

How to connect a custom domain to your Google Sites website

The free address works, but yourbusiness.com conveys more professionalism and is easier to remember. The domain is bought separately from a registrar, and its price depends on each provider and the extension you choose.

General steps

  1. Buy a domain (or use one you already own).
  2. On your site, go to Settings → Custom domains → Start setup.
  3. Enter your domain. If Google asks you to verify that it's yours, do it from Google Search Console by adding the TXT record it gives you in your domain's DNS.
  4. Follow the instructions for your registrar, which usually involve creating a CNAME record in the DNS, and click Done.
  5. Wait: changes can take up to 48 hours, although they are often faster.

What to know beforehand

  • Only the site owner can connect a domain.
  • The site must be published as public.
  • If you delete the site, change its owner or move it to a shared drive, the domain will stop working.
  • With a custom domain, some features, such as certain third-party fonts, may not work the same way.

The exact steps may change. Always follow Google's official guide to using a custom domain.

How to optimise your website to appear on Google

A free website can appear on Google, but there are no guarantees or deadlines. What you can do is make it easy for Google and for your visitors.

Before publishing: the basics

  • Write what your customer would search for. If you are a physiotherapist in Valencia, make "home physiotherapy in Valencia" appear naturally in the title and the first paragraph.
  • A clear name for each page: "Graphic design services" beats "Page 2".
  • Use titles and headings to organise the content.
  • Add alt text to your important images.
  • Make it load fast: don't upload huge images.
  • Make it look good on mobile. Check it in the preview.

After publishing

  • If you have your own domain, verify it in Google Search Console and check which pages Google knows and which searches you appear for.
  • Create and complete your Google Business Profile listing if you have a local business. It helps you appear in searches and on Google Maps.
  • Put your website link on your social media, email signature and business cards.

Be realistic: on Google Sites you can't edit the header code or control technical tags such as each page's meta description, based on what the editor offers today. Google builds the snippet it shows from the visible text, so write a clear, complete first paragraph. Google's official SEO starter guide explains the fundamentals.

SEO tips for entrepreneurs

  1. Define one main search per page and write with that search in mind.
  2. Include your city or area if you work locally.
  3. Answer real customer questions on an FAQ page.
  4. Update your content from time to time: prices, opening hours and services up to date.
  5. Get links from reliable sites: industry directories, associations, customers who mention you.
  6. Don't copy text from other websites: write in your own words.
  7. Be patient. Organic ranking is built over weeks and months, not days.
Being honest

Benefits and limitations of Google Sites

Creating a professional website for free is possible up to a point. It helps to know how far Google Sites goes.

What it does well

To get started

  • Creating and publishing costs nothing.
  • Very easy to use, no technical knowledge.
  • Hosting and maintenance included.
  • Integrates with Forms, Maps, Drive and YouTube.
  • Perfect to test an idea or have a basic presence.
What it lacks

To grow

  • Little control over technical SEO.
  • Design limited to what the editor allows.
  • No built-in blog or online store.
  • Free address includes sites.google.com.
  • Less flexibility for custom integrations.

Frequently asked questions about creating a free website with Google Sites

What entrepreneurs ask us most before getting started.

How do I create a free website?

A simple way is to use Google Sites: sign in with your Google account, choose a blank site or a template, add your text and images and click Publish. You don't need to code or pay for hosting. This guide explains it step by step.

Is it really free to create a website on Google Sites?

Creating and publishing a site on Google Sites costs nothing. The only thing you might pay for is your own domain, for example yourbusiness.com, if you want a more professional address than the one Google gives you.

Can I build a website without coding skills?

Yes. Google Sites works with a visual editor: you insert text boxes, images, buttons and blocks from the Insert menu. If you can use a word processor, you can build a simple website.

Can I use Google Sites for my business or as a freelancer?

Yes, it is a good option for presenting your business, your services and your contact details. It is especially useful to test your idea or get an online presence while you start. If you later need more design control, SEO, or features like an online store or blog, a custom website may suit you better.

Can I connect my own domain to Google Sites?

Yes. From Settings → Custom domains you can connect a domain. Only the site owner can do it, the site must be published as public, and DNS changes can take up to 48 hours. The domain itself is bought separately.

Will my Google Sites page appear on Google?

Google can index a public Google Sites website, but there is no guarantee or timeframe. To help: publish the site as public, write clear texts, give pages descriptive names and submit your address in Google Search Console if you have your own domain.

Can I add a contact form?

Yes. Create a form with Google Forms and add it to your site from Insert → Forms. Responses will arrive in your Google account. You can also add a WhatsApp or email button.

How many pages can I create?

You can add as many pages as you need from the Pages panel. For a business that is starting out, four or five well-crafted pages are usually enough: Home, About, Services, Contact and, optionally, an FAQ.

What are the limitations of Google Sites for a business?

It gives you little control over technical SEO, design is limited to what the editor offers, it has no built-in blog or online store and the free address includes sites.google.com. For a first online presence it works; to grow, a professional website is worth considering.

When should I move from Google Sites to a professional website?

When you want to rank better on Google, build more trust with a custom design, sell online, run a blog or add features such as an AI assistant or bookings. At that point a custom website usually pays off.

Conclusion: start today, improve when you need to

Creating a free website with Google Sites is possible, simple and enough to take your first steps online. With a good structure, clear texts and a visible contact button, you'll already have something to show your customers.

When your business grows, you can step up to a professional website with more design, more SEO and more tools to win customers. And we'll be there to help.
